Pricing Model

How QVeris pricing works

QVeris uses a credits-based model. Discovery and inspection are always free — you only spend credits when your agent makes a capability call.

Discover & Inspect are always free. Your agent can search across 10,000+ capabilities and inspect full parameter schemas, costs, and quality metrics at zero cost.

Credits are consumed per call. Simple data queries cost ~1 credit. Complex operations like PDF parsing (3–10 credits) or financial report analysis (5–15 credits) cost more. Each capability displays its estimated cost during inspection.

Credits never expire. Purchased credits remain available indefinitely. No monthly reset, no use-it-or-lose-it policy.

Pricing depends on your usage profile. The right plan depends on your call volume, capability categories, integration method, and enterprise requirements. All plans include access to the REST API, Python SDK, MCP server, and CLI.

Cost Factors

What affects QVeris pricing

Your cost depends on how your agents use QVeris. Here are the key factors that determine your plan and credit consumption.

📊

Capability volume & categories

Number of capability calls per month and which categories you use. Financial data, document parsing, and real-time APIs have different credit costs based on data depth and provider complexity.

🔌

Integration method

All integration methods — REST API, Python SDK, MCP server, and CLI — are included in every plan. You are not charged differently based on which interface your agent uses.

🛡️

Enterprise requirements

Security, RBAC, audit trails, custom SLAs, compliance support, and dedicated capacity affect enterprise pricing. Contact sales to discuss your organization's specific requirements.

📈

Data provider & sourcing

Capabilities sourced from premium financial data providers or specialized compliance databases may have different credit costs. Each capability shows its provider and estimated cost before you call.

🔍

Observability & audit needs

All plans include execution IDs, session tracking, and credit ledgers. Enterprise plans add custom retention policies, dedicated dashboards, and compliance-ready audit exports.

🏢

Support & governance

Developer and Pro plans include community and email support. Enterprise plans include Enterprise options may include dedicated support, custom integrations, volume discounts, and custom SLAs.
